Refund Policy

Entry fee from $10 to $250 is used to join a tournament. Once tournament is started, entry fee is not refundable.

When Refund Is Possible

If tournament is cancelled by platform, or payment is duplicated by error, refund will be given to same payment method in USD.

Credits

Credits bought or earned are not refundable for cash. Credits can be used for upgrades and power items inside games.

How To Request

Request refund within 7 days with payment proof through website contact option. Decision will be given within 7 working days.
