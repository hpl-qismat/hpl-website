Refund Policy

1. Deposit: Deposit amount is added to your wallet. It can be used to join tournaments.

2. Tournament Cancel: If a tournament is cancelled by HpL before it starts, full entry fee will be refunded to your wallet within 24 hours.

3. After Start: If tournament has started, entry fee is non-refundable.

4. Withdrawal: Withdrawal requests are manually reviewed by Admin. Approved withdrawals are sent to your saved Jazz Cash, Easy Paisa, Bank or Raast account within 1-3 working days.

5. Failed Payment: If payment is deducted but wallet is not updated, contact support with transaction ID. We will verify and refund within 48 hours.

6. Contact: For refund queries, email support@hpl.com
